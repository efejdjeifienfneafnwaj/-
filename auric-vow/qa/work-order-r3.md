# Round 3 Art Review — Work Order

## Verdict

**FAIL** — average score 29.8/100. Five of five lenses failed. The single most damaging problem: four of twenty-one frames (10, 18, 19, 21) render nothing but the DOM HUD over pure black, and `errors.log` is zero bytes, so the pipeline banked them as a clean pass — including both wide composition shots and the skybox shot.

## Blind test summary

All five critics said Warframe wins instantly; three said a player would call it in under a second. The recurring tells: no cast shadows anywhere, gold that reads as tan rubber rope, a back wall stamped as a perfect 20×8 grid, energy VFX made of constant-width tubes and overlapping spheres, and a HUD of opaque rounded rectangles pasted on top of the render.

Harshest (vfx-combat, 24/100):

> "This set was captured to showcase combat VFX and the combat VFX are not in the frames: 09_rifle is a 'muzzle flash, tracers, impacts' shot with no flash, no tracer and no impact; 17_katana is a 'slash arc' shot with no arc; 14/15/16 are three stages of an ultimate that are visually identical and contain no nova. … Warframe's worst marketing still is more legible, better layered and more expensive-looking than this set's best."

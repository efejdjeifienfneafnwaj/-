# Auric Vow — Art Review Work Order

## Verdict
**FAIL** (5/5 critics failed; average score **24.8/100**). Most damaging: the first-person weapon viewmodel is still authored in camera space and drawn over the third-person camera, so two near-plane slabs occlude 25–35% of **every** captured frame — all five critics led with it.

## Blind test summary
All five critics said Warframe wins instantly, and none called it close. Recurring tells: the viewmodel slabs read as a broken build; the level has no cast shadows and no true blacks, so it reads as an untextured greybox with an ivory tint; gold shows no specular ramp; every VFX is one flat additive primitive smeared by bloom; no legible enemy exists in 21 frames. Harshest (ui-readability): *"These frames read as an untextured greybox with a bloom filter and a web-app toast notification in the corner. 12_ability_volley is a flat orange rectangle with a rounded-rect chip floating on it; no shipped game would let that frame out of a capture session."*

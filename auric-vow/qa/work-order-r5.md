# AURIC VOW — R5 Art Review Work Order

## Verdict
**FAIL** (5 of 5 critics failed; average 34.2/100) — raw untextured placeholder geometry is sitting at the near plane in two of nineteen marketing frames (11_ability_dash, 17_katana), with the invisible rifle and the whiteout ultimate close behind.

## Blind test summary
All five lenses said Warframe wins instantly, from a thumbnail, and named the same tells: untextured primitives in the foreground, an ultimate that clips 45-80% of the frame to paper white and takes the HUD numerals with it, a back wall tiled like wallpaper with no lit falloff, gold that is either a black outline or a bloom blob, and a hero frame that reads ~5.5-6 heads with a barrel torso and no neck instead of the 8 heads the code comments claim. Three critics noted the environment is closest to shipping, which makes the character and combat look worse by contrast. Harshest (vfx-combat, 31): *"Put 09_rifle.png or 10_rifle_hits.png next to any Warframe combat still... these frames show a character standing in an empty room with an ammo counter ticking down and literally nothing else happening — a viewer would read it as a bug capture, not a marketing shot... 17_katana.png has an untextured magenta-blotched box cluster filling the bottom third of the screen; that single frame ends the comparison."*

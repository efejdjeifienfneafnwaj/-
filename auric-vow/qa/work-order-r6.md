## Verdict

**FAIL** — all five lenses failed; average score **31.6/100**. Most damaging single problem: the player character, the subject of every frame, is a pale see-through wire armature framed dead-centre at ~22% frame height with a blown-out white chest blob where the silhouette should be — so no shot in the set has a readable subject.

## Blind test summary

All five critics said no, unanimously and without hesitation, and all five said the giveaway lands in under a second. The recurring tells: an indigo/violet sky and periwinkle shadow cast against a reference that has no blue pixel in it; a clean corridor receding to a centred vanishing point with no foreground layer; one ivory-and-gold material family with no dirt, no shadow and no contact darkening anywhere; a HUD of six or seven filled chrome panels where the reference has one; and ability VFX that clip most of the frame to paper white or bolt an opaque cream sphere around the player. Harshest, from the character lens (score 22): *"Beside the reference it reads as a posable robot toy photographed in a set, not as a character in a game."*

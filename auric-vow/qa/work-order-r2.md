## Verdict

**FAIL** — all five lenses failed, average score **27/100**; the fatal problem is that light never interacts with the scene (no cast shadow, no cavity occlusion, no emissive spill, no true black or blown highlight), so every frame reads as an untextured blockout regardless of what is modelled in it.

## Blind test summary

All five critics said Warframe wins instantly, from a thumbnail, without hesitation. The named tells were: a single mid-grey value band with no darks and no controlled highlight; untextured primitive architecture; no light interaction of any kind; separated-primitive character with no hands; and flat single-layer energy VFX. Harshest (vfx-combat, 28): *"A marketing comparison would be read as a parody. This is not 'good for WebGL' territory either — 17_katana is an unshippable frame in any medium."*
